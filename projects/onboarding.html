Turning onboarding friction into business activation.

How I led a customer onboarding redesign that simplified the journey from 13 screens to 6 and improved first-loan activation conversion.

+60% Relative improvement in activation conversion

The challenge

The previous onboarding experience required customers to navigate 13 screens to complete the information and verification steps needed to access credit. The journey was lengthy and made it difficult for customers to understand their progress and what they needed to do next.

The business needed more than a visual refresh. We needed to reduce customer friction and improve the journey from initial signup to the moment a business became activated by taking its first loan.

25% → 40% Activation conversion rate
+15 pp Percentage-point improvement
60% Relative conversion improvement

My approach

I led the redesign around a simple principle: customers should always understand where they are, what is required of them and how each step brings them closer to accessing credit.

Previous experience

13

More screens, a fragmented journey and less clarity about the remaining onboarding requirements.

Redesigned experience

6

A shorter, guided experience with clearer stages, visible progress and more actionable instructions.

01 / SIMPLIFICATION

Reduced unnecessary steps

Consolidated the experience from 13 screens to 6, reducing the complexity customers faced during onboarding.

02 / GUIDANCE

Created a clearer journey

Organised requirements into understandable stages, including personal information, business information, phone and BVN verification, and document submission.

03 / VISIBILITY

Made progress visible

Introduced progress indicators and completion states to help customers understand what they had completed and what remained.

04 / ACTIVATION

Focused on the outcome

Made the next action clearer and aligned the onboarding experience with the business objective: helping customers progress towards their first loan.

The redesigned experience

The new interface gave customers a more structured experience, with clear instructions, visible completion status and a straightforward next action.

Business impact

The redesigned onboarding journey delivered a measurable improvement in the conversion of prospective customers into activated businesses.

25% → 40%

A 60% relative improvement in activation conversion

Activation was defined as a customer taking their first loan. The conversion rate increased by 15 percentage points, representing a 60% relative improvement over the previous rate.

Beyond reducing the number of screens, the redesign made the customer journey easier to understand and gave customers greater visibility into the steps required to progress. It connected a better user experience to a meaningful business outcome.

What this project demonstrates

Product leadership is not just about shipping features. It is about identifying where customers struggle, making deliberate product decisions and connecting improvements in experience to measurable business performance.

This project brought together customer experience design, funnel optimisation, product execution and commercial impact.
