Featured Case Study · Embedded Finance

Making business financing accessible where businesses operate.

Building two embedded finance integration models that enable commerce platforms to offer lending capabilities directly within their ecosystems.

My Role Lead Product Manager
Focus Product Strategy & Delivery
Product Models Widget & Headless API
01 · The Challenge

Financing should meet businesses where they are.

Many small and medium-sized businesses need working capital to grow, manage cash flow, and fulfil customer demand. However, accessing financing through separate platforms can introduce friction into the business journey.

The opportunity was to make financing available within the digital platforms businesses already use, while giving partners flexible ways to integrate lending capabilities.

02 · The Product Vision

One lending capability. Two integration experiences.

I led the vision, strategy, and end-to-end scope for an embedded finance proposition built around two integration models. Each model addressed a different partner need.

01. Embedded Finance Widget

A ready-to-integrate lending experience that allows partners to introduce financing within their existing platform experience without building the entire lending journey themselves.

Partner example: Bumpa

02. Headless Finance API

An API-led model that exposes lending capabilities to partners who want to design and control their own customer experience.

Value: Greater flexibility and custom integration possibilities.

03 · My Leadership & Contribution

From product vision to partner integration.

I owned the product direction and coordinated the work required to translate the opportunity into an integrated product offering.

  • Defined the product vision, strategy, scope, and delivery priorities.
  • Created product requirements documents (PRDs) and technical requirements documents (TRDs).
  • Developed risk matrices and sequence diagrams to clarify dependencies, risks, and system interactions.
  • Defined partner API contract and data consent/complaince requirements.
  • Worked directly with partner contacts to coordinate integration requirements.
  • Aligned engineering, business, and other cross-functional stakeholders throughout delivery.
  • Supported the journey from product definition through integration and launch.
04 · Delivery & Adoption

Turning the proposition into a working partner experience.

The widget-based product was shipped and onboarded by Bumpa. The headless API model also enabled a merchant to integrate lending capabilities into its own experience.

The two models created different routes to market: a ready-made widget for faster partner integration, and a flexible API model for partners requiring a more customised experience.
