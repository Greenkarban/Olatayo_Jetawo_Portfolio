Making financial reconciliation more efficient and reliable.

Contributing to the transformation of reconciliation workflows through business analysis, requirements definition and cross-functional product delivery.

The challenge

Financial reconciliation is a critical operational activity. When transaction information is spread across systems and processes depend on manual checks, teams can spend significant time identifying discrepancies, investigating exceptions and confirming that records align.

The opportunity was to improve the efficiency of reconciliation operations, reduce avoidable manual effort and give teams a more effective way to manage the process.

My contribution

Working across product, business analysis and technical delivery, I helped translate operational challenges into requirements and practical improvements.

Understand the operation

Worked with stakeholders to understand existing reconciliation workflows, pain points, business rules and reporting needs.

Analyse process gaps

Examined current-state processes to identify inefficiencies, manual activities and opportunities to improve workflow execution.

Translate requirements

Converted business needs into functional requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria that technical teams could use.

Support delivery

Collaborated with technical and business stakeholders to clarify requirements, validate functionality and maintain alignment with operational expectations.

The outcome

40–60%

Improvement in reconciliation efficiency

The initiative was associated with a substantial improvement in reconciliation efficiency, helping reduce operational effort and make reconciliation activities more effective.

For me, this project demonstrated the value of connecting detailed process understanding with clear requirements and coordinated technology delivery. The strongest product outcomes often begin with understanding how work actually happens.
