Product Case Study · Lending Infrastructure

Expanding lending capacity through a multi-lender ecosystem.

Developing a lending capability that connects funding partners with lending operations, supporting liquidity, capital recycling, and access to financing.

My Role Lead Product Manager
Ownership Product Vision & Delivery
Delivery MVP Shipped
01 · The Problem

Scaling lending requires more than customer demand.

Lending businesses need access to funding to originate loans and serve customers. Dependence on limited funding sources can constrain lending capacity, slow capital recycling, and affect liquidity.

The opportunity was to develop a multi-lender capability that could connect lending operations with additional funding partners and improve how capital moves through the lending ecosystem.

02 · Product Strategy

Building the capability to work with multiple lenders.

I owned the product vision and end-to-end scope, translating the business opportunity into a product proposition that could support lender partnerships and operational execution.

The core objective was to increase assets under custody and improve the speed at which capital could be recycled into new lending activity.
03 · My Role & Contribution

Connecting business strategy with technical execution.

  • Owned the product vision, scope, and delivery direction.
  • Conducted market research to understand the opportunity and competitive positioning.
  • Contributed to market positioning and go-to-market planning.
  • Created PRDs and technical requirements documents.
  • Developed risk matrices and sequence diagrams to clarify product and system requirements.
  • Defined partner API contracts and data consent requirements.
  • Facilitated stakeholder workshops and coordinated cross-functional execution.
  • Worked with technical and business teams to move the product from definition to MVP.
04 · Delivery

Moving from concept to a working MVP.

The initiative progressed through product definition, technical alignment, stakeholder coordination, and MVP delivery.

Product Definition

Established the product scope, requirements, risks, and partner integration considerations.

Partner Enablement

Advanced the partner model and onboarding approach to support multiple funding relationships.

Cross-functional Delivery

Aligned stakeholders around requirements, dependencies, and execution priorities.

MVP Launch

Shipped the MVP and onboarded Eatonhill Finance as a funding partner.

05 · Business Impact

Creating more room for lending growth.

The MVP established a foundation for working with multiple funding partners. Eatonhill Finance was onboarded, expanding the initiative beyond product development into partner adoption.

  • Expanded the funding partner ecosystem.
  • Supported increased sign-ups and liquidity.
  • Enabled faster capital recycling, based on reported operational outcomes.

Quantitative impact figures are not included here because verified measurements have not yet been established.

Building products around business economics.

It also reflects my experience in fintech product leadership, partner integrations, stakeholder management, and building capabilities that support business scale.
