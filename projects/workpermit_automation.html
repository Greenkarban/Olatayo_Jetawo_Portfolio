The challenge

Work permit processing involves multiple stakeholders, documentation requirements, verification activities and approval stages. When these activities rely on manual coordination, users and administrators can experience delays, repeated follow-ups and limited visibility into application progress.

The initiative focused on streamlining the process through digital workflows, improving how applications moved through the relevant stages and making the experience more efficient for users and operational teams.

My contribution

I worked across business analysis and product delivery to help ensure that the solution reflected real operational needs and could be translated into clear technical requirements.

Map existing processes

Worked with stakeholders to understand application workflows, administrative requirements and process bottlenecks.

Identify improvements

Helped identify opportunities to simplify application handling, reduce manual coordination and improve process visibility.

Translate business needs

Converted operational requirements into functional specifications, user stories and acceptance criteria.

Support implementation

Collaborated with technical and business stakeholders to clarify workflow requirements and support validation of delivered functionality.

The outcome

5,000+ users

The solution supported a user base of more than 5,000 people.

40% faster

Processing time was reduced by approximately 40%, contributing to a more efficient operational experience.

The project demonstrates how thoughtful process analysis and effective collaboration between business and technology teams can help digitise complex enterprise operations and deliver improvements at scale.
