The challenge

Responding to Requests for Proposals can require teams to review lengthy documents, identify relevant requirements, gather information and coordinate responses across multiple contributors.

Repetitive information-gathering and manual preparation can consume valuable presales capacity. The opportunity was to use AI to support this work, helping teams move faster while keeping the response process aligned with business requirements.

My contribution

I supported the product and business analysis work needed to turn presales challenges into a clearly defined AI-enabled solution.

Discover the workflow

Engaged business stakeholders to understand the existing RFP process, recurring activities and points where work slowed down.

Identify automation opportunities

Helped identify repetitive tasks and information bottlenecks that could benefit from AI-assisted workflows.

Define product requirements

Translated business needs into functional requirements and delivery-ready user stories, clarifying expected workflow behaviour.

Align business and technology

Worked with technical and business stakeholders to clarify requirements, review solution expectations and support validation.

The outcome

30%

Reduction in presales turnaround time

The AI-powered RFP automation initiative contributed to faster presales turnaround, helping reduce time spent on repetitive preparation and information-gathering activities.

The project gave me valuable experience translating an emerging technology opportunity into a business-focused solution. The goal was not to introduce AI for its own sake, but to apply it where it could improve the way teams work.
